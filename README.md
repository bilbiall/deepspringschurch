==================================================
2. DESIGN DIRECTION
==================================================

Create a modern, elegant and peaceful editorial-style church website.

The design should communicate:

- Rest
- Spiritual growth
- Community
- Compassion
- Biblical truth
- Service
- Belonging
- Hope
- Genuine relationships

Primary visual character:

- Warm and relational
- Calm and reflective
- Simple but premium
- Community focused
- Faith centred
- Accessible to different generations
- Welcoming to people who have little church experience
- Inclusive of children and people with special needs

Colour palette:

- White as the main background
- Charcoal black for primary text and logo
- Deep spring blue or muted teal as the main accent
- Soft sage green as a secondary accent
- Warm beige, cream or sand for section backgrounds
- Very subtle gold accents where appropriate

Avoid:

- Neon colours
- Excessive gradients
- Heavy black backgrounds
- Overcrowded sections
- Excessive religious symbols
- Flashy stage imagery
- Artificial-looking stock photos
- Wealth or prosperity imagery
- Aggressive giving language
- Manipulative emotional imagery
- Excessive shadows
- Excessive glassmorphism

Typography:

- Use a refined serif font for major headings
- Use a clean, highly readable sans-serif font for body content, menus, buttons and forms
- Headings should feel warm and editorial rather than corporate
- Ensure excellent readability on mobile devices
- Maintain strong contrast between text and backgrounds

Layout:

- Maximum content width of approximately 1200 pixels
- Generous white space
- Clear content hierarchy
- Rounded cards with subtle borders
- Gentle shadows
- Large natural-light photographs
- Spacious mobile layouts
- Consistent section spacing
- Strong visual balance

Use authentic-looking imagery showing:

- People walking together
- Small groups
- Families
- Prayer
- Worship
- Community service
- Mentoring
- Conversations
- Hospitality
- People of different ages and backgrounds

Avoid celebrity pastor imagery, massive stages, luxury, money imagery or highly staged photography.

6. ABOUT US PAGE
==================================================

Page heading:

OUR STORY AND PURPOSE

Include the following church background:

The Church of the Nazarene is a Protestant Christian church in the Wesleyan-Holiness tradition. Its roots are traced to 1908, and it was founded to spread the message of scriptural holiness, meaning Christlike living, across the world. Today, the Church of the Nazarene is located in 156 world areas.

The Church of the Nazarene is a Great Commission church. Its mission is to make Christlike disciples in the nations.

We believe God offers forgiveness, peace, joy, purpose, love, meaning in life and the promise of eternal life through a personal relationship with God through Jesus Christ. We are called to take this message to people everywhere.

The Church of the Nazarene is also a holiness church. We believe Christians can experience a deeper life with God marked by victory over sin, power to witness and serve, and richer fellowship with God through the infilling of the Holy Spirit.

- Maintain good mobile performance
